InkTide: Territory Dash

Privacy Policy · Effective 19 October 2026

Summary

Solo play: no data sent

InkTide: Territory Dash ("the app") can be played fully offline on your own, and also online against other players if you choose. Solo play does not collect or transmit anything. Online play sends only what is needed to run the match (see “Online play” below). Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device, in Android's local app storage:

  • Game settings (sound, music, haptic feedback, game speed)
  • Game progress (levels completed, coins earned, skins unlocked, best results)

This data stays on your device and the developer cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with Reset progress in the app's Settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Online play (optional)

Online play only starts when you tap Play Online. Solo play never connects to anything.

When you play online, the app connects to our game server and sends:

  • your nickname (you choose it; up to 14 characters, using letters, numbers, spaces, _, . and -; shown to the other players in the same arena),
  • the skin you have equipped,
  • your steering input while you play,
  • a player ID: your Google Play Games player ID if you signed in with Play Games, otherwise a random guest ID created on your device,
  • and, like any internet connection, your IP address (the server and its hosting provider see it).

The server uses this only to run the match. It keeps it in memory while you are in the arena and does not write it to disk or a database; it is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for analytics. The hosting provider may keep ordinary network logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy. Connections are encrypted (wss://) when you use the official server.

Other players in your arena see your nickname and skin. Nicknames pass through a basic word filter, and a nickname that fails it is replaced with a generic name. Please do not use your real name or personal details as a nickname.

Google Play Games sign-in is optional and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We receive your Play Games player ID and display name. You can sign out in the Play Games app.

Deleting your data: because the server stores nothing after you leave, there is nothing to delete there. Your nickname and guest ID on your device are removed when you use Reset progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall.

Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few friendly reminders on your device (for example about rewards or the daily skin deal). They are created and shown by your phone itself: nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The game cancels them whenever you open it, and they stop about a week after your last visit. You can turn them off in the game's Settings (“Reminders”) or in Android's notification settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account is required (Google Play Games sign-in is optional)
  • No internet connection is used for solo play
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files

Children

InkTide is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information through online play, contact us below and we will delete it.

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of operating Google Play (for example, install statistics) under Google's Privacy Policy. This is outside the app's control.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
